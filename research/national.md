# National research plan

Last updated: November 22, 2026

## How big is this?

- IBISWorld estimated **3,889 strip club businesses** in the U.S. in 2025, with about **$4.2 billion** in revenue and 35,639 employees. That employee count likely leaves out dancers classified as contractors. IBISWorld describes the industry as fragmented, with no company holding a large share, and expects revenue to keep declining.
- The largest public operator, **RCI Hospitality Holdings** (Nasdaq: RICK), ran about 59 adult clubs in 15 states at the end of fiscal 2025.
- Sources: [IBISWorld, number of businesses](https://www.ibisworld.com/industry-statistics/number-of-businesses/strip-clubs-united-states), [IBISWorld industry report](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-research-reports/strip-clubs-industry), [RCI FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/935419/000162828026019804/rick-20250930.htm)
