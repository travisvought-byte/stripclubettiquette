## Ohio law in brief

- [ORC 2907.40](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2907.40): sexually oriented businesses close from midnight to 6 a.m. (liquor-permit holders can stay open later only without nude entertainment), and touching between nude or semi-nude employees and customers is a misdemeanor.
- [ORC 5502.63](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5502.63): the state encourages, but doesn't require, adult entertainment establishments to post the human trafficking hotline poster.
- Local licensing and zoning vary. Example: [Cincinnati Code Chapter 899](https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/finance/linkservid/8D35B185-003D-BB43-494824B44B44EAA1/showMeta/0/).
- More in [guides/laws-by-state.md](../guides/laws-by-state.md#ohio).

## Best sources for verifying clubs

1. Ohio Division of Liquor Control permit lookup (status by business name or address)
2. Ohio Liquor Control Commission orders (fines, revocations)
3. City and township adult-business licenses, such as Cincinnati's Chapter 899 licenses
4. Federal and county court dockets (S.D. Ohio and N.D. Ohio for wage suits)
5. Local news: Dayton Daily News, Cleveland Scene, Columbus Dispatch, WOSU, WTOL, News 5 Cleveland, Toledo Blade
6. Club websites and social pages for hours and status

## Key Ohio cases

- **Doe 1 et al. v. VM3015, Inc. et al.** (S.D. Ohio No. 2:18-cv-443), filed May 6, 2018. Alleges Ohio clubs agreed to pay dancers no wages while charging them fees. The defendant list is a useful lead list (see below). Source: [ClassAction.org](https://classaction.org/news/ohio-gentlemens-clubs-accused-of-colluding-to-decrease-dancers-wages)
- **$800,000 dancer settlement (2026).** Cleveland Ave. Restaurant, Inc. (the company behind Sirens) and related defendants agreed to pay $800,000 to dancers who worked at six clubs between May 14, 2014 and June 16, 2025: Cheeks (West Carrollton), Private Dancer (Columbus), Top Hat (Mansfield), House of Babes (Columbus), Fantasyland West (Bucyrus) and Sirens (Columbus). Settlement summaries describe a standardized lease that treated dancers as tenants, and name two Ohio club trade groups among the defendants. The claims deadline was October 20, 2026. This is probably the Hogan case below; confirm the caption on the docket. Sources: [ClaimDepot](https://www.claimdepot.com/settlements/ohio-strip-club-case), [OpenClassActions](https://openclassactions.com/settlements/ohio-nightclub-dancer-unpaid-wages-class-action-settlement.php), [AllAboutLawyer](https://allaboutlawyer.com/cleveland-ave-restaurant-800000-dancers-settlement/)
- **Hogan v. Cleveland Ave Restaurant, Inc. d/b/a Sirens** (S.D. Ohio No. 2:15-cv-2883). Bartender and entertainer classes; 2020 court orders address a settlement fund. Source: [court order](https://docs.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/ohio/ohsdce/2:2015cv02883/188214/308)
- **Class certification, September 2023.** Law360 reported that an Ohio federal judge certified a class of dancers in a suit claiming a group of Ohio clubs, their owners and an industry figure conspired over dancer pay, with dancers paying "rent" to work. Confirm which of the cases above this is. Source: [Law360](https://law360.com/articles/1718666)

## Enforcement patterns

- **Liquor permits are the main lever.** Dayton-area undercover investigations in 2017 and 2018 led to permit revocations and a closure (Sharkey's, The Harem). The state denied a permit renewal for Club Plush. Akron and Columbus have objected to renewals for clubs with heavy police calls.
- **Nuisance actions close problem venues,** including an unlicensed east Columbus strip club that operated behind a lingerie storefront and was shut down by the city in 2026. Source: [ABC6](https://abc6onyourside.com/news/local/underground-stip-club-fronting-as-lingerie-store-shut-down-by-city)
