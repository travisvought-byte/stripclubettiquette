# Club Safety Standard

A checklist any club can meet. It's built from Washington State's 2024 strip club law, federal anti-trafficking guidance, and the practices that keep clubs out of court.

Version 0.1 (draft), November 22, 2026

A club that adopts the [Model policies and procedures](../guides/model-policies.md) template covers most of Level 1 in writing.

Clubs can self-report (shown as "self-reported") or provide evidence (shown as "verified"). Evidence can be written policies, photos, training records, or a site visit.

## Level 1: Baseline

### Dancer safety
- [ ] At least one dedicated security worker on duty during all open hours, watching dancer-customer interactions.
- [ ] Panic buttons in every room where a dancer may be alone with a customer, plus dressing rooms and restrooms, tested at least monthly.
- [ ] Dressing rooms locked by keypad or equivalent.
- [ ] Written procedure for abusive or violent customers, covering ejection, bans, and when to call police.
- [ ] Private dance areas visible to staff or covered by cameras. No locking doors.

### Fair pay and transparency
- [ ] One written fee schedule, the same for every dancer, handed over before the first shift.
- [ ] No late fees, missed-shift fines, interest, or debt carried forward.
- [ ] Tips belong to the dancer, and a sign in the dressing room says so.
- [ ] Dancers paid directly, never through a third party.

### Anti-trafficking
- [ ] Every entertainer's government ID checked in person before the first shift. Entertainers keep their own IDs.
- [ ] All staff trained on trafficking indicators and reporting, at hire and every year.
- [ ] National and state hotline posters in dressing rooms and restrooms, in English and Spanish.
- [ ] One named manager responsible for trafficking concerns, with written escalation steps.
- [ ] Written zero-tolerance policy on prostitution and on staff steering customers to "extras."

### Customer fairness
- [ ] Posted prices for dances, VIP time, and any dance-dollar surcharge or ATM fee.
- [ ] Itemized receipts for every card charge.

## Level 2: Best practice

- [ ] Fees at or below Washington's cap (per 8 hours: the lesser of $150 or 30% of earnings outside private areas, plus 30% of private-area earnings), or no fees at all.
- [ ] Worker classification reviewed by employment counsel within the last 2 years.
- [ ] A ban list for abusive customers, shared across commonly owned clubs.
- [ ] Written reasons for any termination within 10 business days.
- [ ] A way to report problems that doesn't run through the dancer's direct manager, and a written no-retaliation policy.
- [ ] Responsible alcohol service training for all servers and bartenders.
- [ ] De-escalation and first-aid training for security and managers.
- [ ] A working relationship with a local anti-trafficking coalition or victim-services group.
- [ ] Escorts to cars on request.

## How it shows up in the directory

- Each item a club documents becomes a `positive` record in [data/evidence.csv](../data/evidence.csv).
- A club that meets every Level 1 item shows "Meets Club Safety Standard, Level 1," marked self-reported or verified.
